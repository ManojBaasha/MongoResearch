### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Showing empty states** - Judges can't tell if it works
❌ **Using placeholder data** - Need real grants, papers, news
❌ **Rushing through demo** - Spend time showing it works
❌ **Not testing before recording** - Test everything first!
❌ **Unclear MongoDB mention** - Must be clear and visible
❌ **Poor audio quality** - Can hurt pitch score
❌ **Technical jargon without visuals** - Match words to what's shown
